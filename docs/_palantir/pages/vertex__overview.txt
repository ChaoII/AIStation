Vertex
Vertex
allows you to visualize and quantify cause and effect across the digital twin of your real-world organization. Using the Vertex toolkit, you can access and explore existing graphs that have been curated and published by your organization or build new system graphs.
Informing data-driven decisions based on current, predicted, or proposed conditions, Vertex provides a toolkit to monitor, simulate, and optimize operational decision-making to maximize organizational outcomes.
Vertex can help you:
Bring transparency
by exploring a live view of your whole network across functional silos.
Focus on what matters
with central alerting for top risks and opportunities and how they influence targets.
Simulate the future
by combining analytics and operations to find the most impactful changes you can make.
Learn and optimize
by improving data clarity and model accuracy to see what is working and work towards a global optimum.
